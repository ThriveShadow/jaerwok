    3. NGRDI vegetation index
    
      
    Computes the Normalized Green-Red Difference Index (NGRDI) from the orthomosaic to
        highlight plant vigor and canopy health. Produces a raw single-band GeoTIFF plus a Red-Yellow-Green
        colorized GeoTIFF, and a light preview PNG for quick viewing.
