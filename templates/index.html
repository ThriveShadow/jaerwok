    4. YOLOv8 detection
    
      
    Runs a YOLOv8 model over the orthomosaic. The image is chunked into
        overlapping tiles for CPU inference (handles large orthophotos without loading the
        full image into memory), then detections are de-duplicated and drawn onto a single
        annotated JPEG.
